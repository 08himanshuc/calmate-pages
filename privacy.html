Privacy Policy
Last Updated: June 18, 2026

Welcome to CalMate ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our CalMate mobile application (the "App") and our related services.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access or use the application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the App depends on the features you use and includes:

A. Personal Data

  • Account Registration Details: Email address, display name, and profile photo when you sign in or create an account via Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, or Sign-In with Apple.

B. Fitness, Meal, and Nutrition Data

  • Nutritional Logs: Logged food items, ingredient descriptions, portion sizes, calorie counts, and macronutrients (protein, carbs, fats).
  • Preferences & Goals: Caloric goals, physical parameters (if input), and dietary preferences (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Keto, Halal, Gluten-Free).

C. Device Permissions & Media

  • Camera & Photo Gallery Access: We request access to your device's camera and photo library to allow you to log meals instantly using image recognition.
  • Microphone & Audio Access: We request access to your microphone to allow you to dictate or record voice descriptions for recipes and meals.
  • Device Information: Mobile device ID, model, manufacturer, operating system version, and IP address.

D. Purchase & Subscription Data

  • Transaction Reference: Subscription status and transaction identifiers. All financial transactions are processed securely via Google Play Services, Apple App Store, and RevenueCat. We do not store or collect your payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our App. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Create and secure your CalMate account.
  • Process images and voice entries using AI models (such as the Google Gemini API) to generate recipe suggestions, nutritional breakdowns, and meal plans.
  • Track your daily nutritional progress and generate charts/statistics.
  • Manage and verify your premium subscriptions through RevenueCat.
  • Respond to support inquiries and improve user experience.
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and monitor against theft or unauthorized activity.

Data Processing Notice: Captured food images and audio recordings are processed via secure APIs (such as Google Gemini API) for the sole purpose of analyzing nutritional value and generating plans. We do not sell your personal media or data to third-party advertisers.

3. Sharing & Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with service providers who perform operations on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase: Used for account authentication, user management, and secure cloud database storage (Cloud Firestore).
  • Google Gemini API: Used to analyze meal photos, transcribe/interpret recipes, and generate personalized nutrition guidelines.
  • RevenueCat: Used to coordinate and sync subscription states across platforms.
  • App Stores: Payment processors of Google (Google Play Store) and Apple (Apple App Store) handle billing transactions securely.

4. Data Retention and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data. Your account information and nutritional logs are stored securely using Google Cloud / Firestore. We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide you with our services. You can request the deletion of your account and associated data directly within the App settings or by contacting us.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately from our servers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
