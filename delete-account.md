# Account Deletion Request

**CalMate** (developed by **WeMate Labs**) provides a simple way for users to request the deletion of their account and all associated personal data. This policy is designed to comply with Google Play's Data Safety requirements.

---

## How to Request Account Deletion

You can request the deletion of your account and associated data using either of the following methods:

### Method 1: Email Request (Recommended)
1. Send an email to **[wematelabs@gmail.com](mailto:wematelabs@gmail.com)**.
2. Use the subject line: **"Account Deletion Request - CalMate"**.
3. Include the email address associated with your CalMate account.
4. Your request will be processed, and your account and data will be fully deleted within **7 business days**. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

### Method 2: In-App Deletion
1. Open the **CalMate** app on your device.
2. Go to the **Profile** or **Settings** screen.
3. Tap on **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm your choice. This will immediately trigger the deletion of your authentication account.

---

## Data Deletion & Retention Details

When your account deletion request is processed:

### What is Permanently Deleted
* **Account Credentials:** Your email, display name, and profile pictures associated with your Firebase, Google, or Apple login profile.
* **Nutritional & Fitness Logs:** All logged food items, meals, calories, macronutrients, and goals history.
* **Dietary Preferences:** Saved diets (Keto, Halal, Vegan, etc.) and customized configurations.
* **Device Identifiers:** Any saved device tokens or push notification settings linked to your profile.

### What is Kept (and why)
* **Transaction History:** Records of subscriptions or in-app purchases processed via Apple/Google Play billing and RevenueCat are retained. These records are kept strictly for financial accounting, tax verification, and legal compliance. No personal identifying information (PII) is kept with these records.
* **Anonymized Analytics:** Aggregated usage statistics (e.g., app crash reports, feature usage rates) may be retained. This data is entirely anonymized and cannot be traced back to any individual user.
